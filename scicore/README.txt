Beluga MAGs mapping, depth extraction, and MAG-level coverage summarisation scripts.
Scripts correspond to the methods described in the ISME Communications manuscript(Carrillo-Barragan P., et al.2026):
- Bowtie2 indexing
- Read mapping
- SAM→BAM conversion
- Depth extraction
- MAG-level coverage aggregation

These files were used to produce the mapping and coverage results for the Beluga River Rhine dataset.
